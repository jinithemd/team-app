Facts: Mike, a Wyandotte rooster, Fruita, Colorado. Lloyd Olsen tried to behead him on 10 Sep 1945 for a dinner with his mother-in-law. Much of the brain stem and one ear survived. Mike was fed liquid food and water with an eyedropper. He toured sideshows, appeared in Time and Life magazines, and earned about $4,500 a month at his peak. He choked and died in a Phoenix motel in March 1947 because the feeding syringe had been left behind at the previous stop. Fruita has held the Mike the Headless Chicken Festival since 1999.
