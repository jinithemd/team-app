Facts (how strongly to say each one):
- Real: Natwarlal (Mithilesh Kumar Srivastava, from Bihar) was a forger and con man. He forged signatures, including the President's, posed as a government official and cheated many people. He was convicted in many cases with total sentences reported as 113 years. He escaped custody many times (usually given as 8 or 9). His last disappearance was in 1996, aged about 84, at New Delhi railway station while being taken to hospital. In 2009 his lawyer said he had died that year; his brother said he had died in 1996.
- Legend: the Taj Mahal, Red Fort, Rashtrapati Bhavan and Parliament "sales" are the famous story about him and can't be proven. That's why line 5 says "എന്നാണ് പറയപ്പെടുന്നത്" (it is said).
- Popular story: the police-uniform escape. Keep it as told.
