Facts: Violet Jessop (1887-1971). RMS Olympic collided with HMS Hawke on 20 Sep 1911. Titanic sank 15 Apr 1912 (she was in lifeboat 16 and was handed a baby). HMHS Britannic hit a mine on 21 Nov 1916 and sank in under an hour. She jumped from a lifeboat being drawn into the propellers and later said she had fractured her skull. She retired in 1950 and died in 1971, aged 83.
